# US-Healthcare-Data-Analysis
# Strategic Healthcare Analytics: Optimizing Operations & Finance

## Project Overview
This project provides a data-driven analysis of hospital operations, focusing on patient demographics, billing optimization, and resource management. I identified key cost drivers and operational bottlenecks to recommend strategic action plans for healthcare efficiency.

##  Key Insights & Problem Statements
I addressed three primary strategic areas:
1. [cite_start]**Demographic Analysis:** Understanding patient health profiles to tailor care[cite: 46].
2. [cite_start]**Patient Price Optimization:** Identifying high-cost drivers (like Diabetes costing 28% above average) to ensure fair billing[cite: 47, 58].
3. [cite_start]**Resource Management:** Improving patient throughput and "Length of Stay" (LOS) optimization[cite: 48].

##  Analytical Highlights
* [cite_start]**Condition Prevalence:** Hypertension was identified as the most prevalent condition, particularly among males[cite: 50, 52].
* [cite_start]**Cost Drivers:** Analysis revealed that **Diabetes** and **Cancer** pathways are the highest financial burdens[cite: 58, 59].
* [cite_start]**Billing Equity:** Verified that average billing is stable across top payers (UnitedHealthcare, Aetna, Cigna) with minimal variance (approx. 5%)[cite: 79, 80].
* [cite_start]**Operational Efficiency:** Identified that **Emergency admissions** have significantly longer stays compared to Elective admissions, which are 39% more efficient[cite: 82, 83].
